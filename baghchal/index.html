🎯 How to Play Bagh-Chal

🎮 Objective

  • Tigers 🐅: Capture 5 goats to win
  • Goats 🐐: Trap all 4 tigers so they cannot move

🧩 Game Phases

Phase 1: Placement Phase

  • Goat places one piece → Tiger moves → Repeat
  • Goats place one at a time on any empty intersection
  • After each goat placement, tigers can move one step
  • Tigers can also capture during placement phase
  • Continue alternating until all 20 goats are placed

Phase 2: Movement Phase

  • Begins after all 20 goats are placed
  • Both goats and tigers can move
  • Turns continue to alternate
  • Game continues until win condition is met

🐐 Goat Rules

  • Placement: Click any empty point to place
  • Movement: Click a goat, then an adjacent empty point
  • Can only move one step along connected lines
  • Cannot jump or capture tigers
  • Work together to trap all tigers

🐅 Tiger Rules

  • Move one step along connected lines
  • Capture by jumping over a goat to an empty space
  • Can only jump in straight lines (horizontal, vertical, diagonal)
  • Only one goat per jump

⚖️ Game Flow

  • Goats always move first
  • Turns alternate between goats and tigers
  • Game ends when tigers capture 5 goats OR all tigers are trapped
